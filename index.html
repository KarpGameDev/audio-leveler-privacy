Privacy Policy

Audio processing

Audio Leveler processes website audio locally in your browser to adjust volume, reduce sudden volume changes, and apply the audio settings you select. If you explicitly enable tab capture, it processes the selected tab's audio in a background document and plays the processed audio locally. It does not record audio to files or upload audio to the developer or subscription services. Audio-level measurements and visualization history are held temporarily for processing and display. The extension does not request microphone or camera access.

Information stored on your device

The extension stores settings in browser extension storage, including volume settings, presets, language, theme, audio feature preferences, and optional parental-control settings and PIN. It can store website hostnames with the audio profiles you choose for those sites. This information is used to apply your settings, not to build a browsing-history profile.

If you connect a subscription account, local storage also contains account and entitlement information, such as the provider, user ID, display name, membership or tier identifiers, subscription status, and verification timestamps. Boosty subscription information can include an expiration date. Temporary sign-in state is stored to complete authentication. The selected theme is also mirrored in localStorage.

If you use settings export, a file containing extension storage data is saved to the location you choose. It may contain account metadata and the parental-control PIN. Keep exported files private.

Patreon authentication and verification

Patreon sign-in is optional and starts when you choose it. The extension uses Patreon's OAuth authorization flow. The authorization code and redirect address are sent over HTTPS to the subscription backend:
https://extension-subscription.karpgamedev.workers.dev/

The backend exchanges the code with Patreon and queries Patreon for account identity, campaign ownership, and membership information to determine access. Patreon credentials are handled by the backend; the current session flow returns account and access information to the extension rather than provider tokens. An encrypted, secure, HttpOnly session cookie maintains the backend session. Older installations may submit a previously stored refresh token to the backend when migrating to this session flow.

Boosty authentication and cookies

Boosty sign-in is optional. The cookies permission is requested when you choose Boosty. The extension reads the auth cookie for boosty.to to obtain the existing session's access token, then sends authenticated requests directly to api.boosty.to to verify account identity and subscription eligibility for the karpdev creator page. The token is not copied into extension storage or sent to the Patreon backend. Changes to the Boosty authentication cookie are monitored to update sign-in status.

External services and sharing

Patreon, Boosty, and Cloudflare, which hosts the subscription backend, process information necessary to provide authentication, subscription verification, and network delivery. These services receive normal connection information, such as an IP address, and may process operational or security logs under their own policies. Website audio, parental-control settings, and website audio profiles are not sent to the subscription backend.

The extension does not include advertising or third-party analytics trackers. The developer does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Payment details are handled by Patreon or Boosty, not by the extension. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

Retention and your choices

Local settings and account metadata remain until replaced, cleared through the extension's reset controls, or removed with the extension. Backend session cookies expire and are cleared through the Patreon logout flow. Logging out does not cancel a paid subscription or delete your Patreon or Boosty account. You can revoke optional permissions in Chrome, revoke Patreon authorization through Patreon, and manage subscriptions through the relevant provider. Provider records and infrastructure logs follow the relevant service's retention policies.

You may use the extension's basic audio features without connecting a subscription account. Uninstalling the extension removes its extension storage; downloaded export files and provider-side records must be managed separately.

Security

Subscription requests use HTTPS. Server secrets are not included in the extension. Access is limited to information needed for the features described above. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.
